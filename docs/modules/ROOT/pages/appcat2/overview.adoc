= Helmetica Overview

Helmetica turns one Helm chart into a service that users can order many instances of.
Three roles work with it, and each one touches different components.
Find your role below to see what you interact with.

The names come from alchemy, see the xref:appcat2/servicemaintainer/glossary.adoc[glossary] for every term.

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Role | Job | You work with

| <<operator,Framework operator>>
| Runs the framework on a cluster and provides the platform it relies on.
| Chrysopoeia, adept, sigillum, ampulla, custos, libra, aludel, FluxCD

| <<maintainer,Service maintainer>>
| Wraps an upstream chart and publishes it as a service.
| Transmuter, ferment, azoth, athanor, reagent, claim definition

| <<user,User>>
| Orders and operates instances of a service.
| Claim, action, cupel
|===

[#how-it-fits]
== How the pieces fit

A reagent is a Helm chart.
It renders small, platform-agnostic resources next to the service, and one controller per topic turns each of them into the real thing.
The service maintainer never talks to these controllers directly.
Backup, network, maintenance and billing are switched on in the reagent's `values.yaml` and rendered by azoth.
Credentials are defined in the reagent's own `templates/credentials.yaml`.

[#operator]
== Framework operator

The operator keeps the framework and the platform it needs running.
They don't write charts or order services.

* *Installs the controllers.* Chrysopoeia, sigillum, ampulla, adept, custos and libra each have their own repository.
  In sigillum, custos and libra the CRDs are installed separately from the controllers.
* *Provides the platform.* FluxCD deploys the charts, a COSI driver (for example aludel-cloudscale) hands out buckets, K8up runs backups, and Gateways serve network exposures.
  Sigillum never creates Gateways.
* *Describes the platform.* Config maps annotated with `chrysopoeia.io/platform-values` end up under `__platform__` in every instance's values, so charts can adapt to the distribution or load balancer capabilities.
  Adept's `MaintenanceWindow` objects define when maintenance may start for all instances.

[#maintainer]
== Service maintainer

The maintainer turns an upstream chart into a service and ships it.

. The xref:appcat2/servicemaintainer/glossary.adoc#transmuter[transmuter] scaffolds a reagent from ferment and adds the upstream chart (prima materia) as a dependency.
. Ferment brings the azoth library chart along, which renders the backup, network, maintenance, alerting, monitoring and billing resources from the reagent's `values.yaml`.
  Ferment also ships a `templates/credentials.yaml` to fill in with the service's connection details.
. You adjust the values, rituals and credentials in the reagent, and try it in athanor, a local development cluster.
. Assay checks the chart and detects breaking CRD changes against the latest published version. Touchstone tests it end to end.
. You publish the chart to an OCI registry and register it with a `ClaimDefinition`.
  It points at a FluxCD `OCIRepository` for the chart and an `ImageRepository` to discover new versions.
  Chrysopoeia then generates the CRD users order from.

[#user]
== User

The user only deals with what the maintainer made available.
They don't need to know about charts or the controllers behind them.

. The user creates a *claim*, an instance of the CRD generated from the service's chart.
  It sets the version, the configuration and the approval strategy.
. Every change to the claim is recorded as a revision, and the latest *approved* one is deployed as a FluxCD `HelmRelease` into the instance's own namespace, which chrysopoeia creates.
  Unless the claim's approval strategy is `Automatic`, a new revision waits until someone approves it.
. The service's credentials appear in a secret.
. To run an operation such as a restart, the user creates an *action*, and adept runs it as a job in the instance namespace.
. Cupel shows what a change would do before it is approved (`weigh`), and browses an instance's revision history (`ledger`).
